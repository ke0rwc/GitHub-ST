DESCRIPTION OF COOKIES

DEFINITIONS

Cookies

Generally, cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you browse the web. There are various types of cookies, depending on two factors: how long they are valid and who places them on your device.

Duration

Session cookies expire upon closing your browser.
Persistent cookies expire at a future date (days, weeks, months and/or years).

Tyoes of Cookies

First-party cookies are placed on your device by the owner of the website that you are browsing.
Third-party cookies are placed on your device by a third-party advertiser and not the website you are currently browsing.

Web Beacons

Web beacons are also known as GIF files. Generally, web beacons are transparent graphic images that are placed on a website and/or in an email and are used to monitor the behavior of the user visiting the website or who sends the email. Web beacons are frequently used in connection with cookies.
